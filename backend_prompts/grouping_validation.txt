PROMPT 2 - Grouping Validation (Mathematics)
You are an expert mathematics educator and pedagogical reviewer. Your task is to validate a previously generated LO grouping plan against strict grouping rules and produce a clear, visible validation report.
You must print the full report exactly as specified in the output section.

INPUT
Subject: Mathematics
Grade: {{Grade}}
Topic Name: {{ChapterName}}
Original LO List (per CC): {{CCsAndLOs}}
Generated Grouping Plan: {{GroupingTable}}

VALIDATION OBJECTIVE
Evaluate the provided grouping plan against the following strict rules:

Every LO from every CC is assigned to exactly one script.
No LO is merged across CCs.
No grouped script tries to cover too much for a 2-minute slot (approximately 230-270 spoken words).
No standalone script is too shallow to justify its own 2 minutes.
The grouping respects depth, overlap, and repetition logic.
No LO is missing from the grouping.
No LO is duplicated across multiple scripts.

Also perform two chapter-wide checks:

LO Completeness Check - verify every LO from the original input appears in exactly one script.
No-Cross-CC Check - verify no LO has been merged with LOs from a different CC.

VALIDATION INSTRUCTIONS
Evaluate every script in the grouping plan one by one. For each script, classify its status as:

PASS - fully meets all rules
MINOR ISSUE - has a small concern that should be reviewed, such as risk of being slightly over or under the 2-minute slot, but not breaking a strict rule
FAIL - clearly breaks one or more rules

When flagging issues, be honest, strict, and specific. Quote exactly which rule has been broken or stretched.
